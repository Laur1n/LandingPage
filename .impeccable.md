# Design Context — Laurin Wünsch

> Established via `impeccable teach` (August 2026). Updated for Editorial Bauhaus (forms &
> symbols over functions). Do not reuse another client's brand tokens.

## Users

- **Primary — Cue Pilot audience**: Fitness-Instruktorinnen and -Instruktoren of group classes,
  studio owners, and teams who need to plan courses, manage timing/cues, and keep music and flow
  in one place. Often browsing on mobile between classes or during planning — they need to
  immediately understand what Cue Pilot does and how to try it.
- **Secondary — IT clients**: Decision-makers evaluating Laurin for digital product work or
  consulting. Desktop, professional context — credibility and clarity matter; no corporate gray
  sterility.
- **Secondary — sport & wellness clients**: Individuals booking training, or venues (hotels,
  thermen) booking Aufguss experiences. Warmth and trust over flash.

Job to be done, in one line: _discover Cue Pilot and request a demo_ — while coming away with a
clear sense that Laurin builds precise, human-centered tools (and brings the same mindset to sport
and wellness).

## Brand Personality

Three concrete words: **precise, warm, calm** — expressed as **geometric · editorial · rhythmic**.

From Laurin's positioning: *„Gute Lösungen entstehen, wenn Technik und Mensch zusammenpassen.“*
Cue Pilot is the flagship proof: instructors, bodies, rhythm, groups — not abstract users.

Emotional goal: **confident calm** with Bauhaus clarity — no hype, no SaaS theater.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual tone**: Editorial Bauhaus — geometric type, asymmetric magazine layouts, forms and
  symbols over UI chrome. Muted Bauhaus primaries (blue / yellow / red) carry intensity and accent.
- **Theme**: Light (paper) and dark (black primary surface) via `data-theme` + header toggle.
  Same Bauhaus system; dark `--color-surface` is black (`oklch(0% 0 0)`).
- **Primary action**: Hero leads with Cue Pilot meaning; interactive symbol-instrument (BPM, bars,
  draggable playhead, session circle) mocks features without app-kit chrome.
- **Anti-reference**: NOT SaaS dark-hero + pill CTAs + glass nav. NOT fitness neon, spa stock,
  consulting gray, or Francesca warm-jazz editorial.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA; Atkinson Hyperlegible body; Archivo display.

## Design Principles

1. **One cast** — paper, ink, 2px rules, zero radius, Bauhaus primary triad everywhere.
2. **Cue Pilot first** — meaning before chrome; symbol-instrument over fake app UI.
3. **Forms over functions** — circles, bars, numerals carry intensity and beat.
4. **Editorial hierarchy** — Archivo display + Atkinson body; asymmetric grids.
5. **No genre costumes** — sport/wellness stay geometric, not neon or spa.

## Design Tokens (source of truth for implementation)

### Typography

- **Display**: Archivo Variable — geometric grotesque, editorial weight range.
- **Body**: Atkinson Hyperlegible — calm, mobile-legible.
- Fluid `clamp()` headings; ≥1.25 step ratio; body max-width ~65ch.

### Color (OKLCH)

Light paper / dark black. Never pure `#fff`; dark surface may be pure black.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--color-surface` | `oklch(96% 0.012 85)` | `oklch(0% 0 0)` | Page |
| `--color-surface-raised` | `oklch(92% 0.016 85)` | `oklch(12% …)` | Raised bands |
| `--color-ink` | `oklch(18% 0.02 255)` | `oklch(94% …)` | Text / rules |
| `--color-bauhaus-blue/yellow/red` | muted triad | brighter triad | Intensity + CTA |

`--radius: 0`. Footer uses `--color-surface-product`.

### Spacing

4pt semantic scale: `--space-xs` … `--space-4xl`.

### Motion

Transform/opacity only; expo-out; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables beat timing.

### Explicit bans

No side-stripe accents, no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no pill CTAs, no SaaS glow heroes,
no identical icon-card grids.
